Don't listen to yourself. Start talking to yourself. Too many times during a match when we're in that chair, we're starting to feel sorry for ourselves. We start to focus on the bad roles, the negatives. This is a sign that you're on the crossroads. Keep listening and you can spiral out of control very easily and very fast. Or you can start talking to yourself in a constructive, helpful way. Start with a couple of nice deep breaths to supply your brain with enough quality oxygen. And then have a couple of words ready written down or in your mind that you can look at or repeat in your mind. No more than three words. For example, soft, ready, composed, or focus, trust, attack, or how about respect, humble, focus, you name it. Choose three words that have a strong meaning for you. Start talking to yourself today and stop listening. Go for it. Good luck.
