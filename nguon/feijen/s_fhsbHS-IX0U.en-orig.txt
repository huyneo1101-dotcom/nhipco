When you're struggling in a match, don't compare your worst game to your best game. This internal chatter right here can sound very familiar. Why does it have to be so tough? I shouldn't have to work for it so much. I'm better than this. Why am I underperforming so badly or even my best game is nowhere near this? You see, first of all, all these thoughts are taking you away from the present moment. And secondly, you're creating a huge gap between your current game and your best game. Look, you're the rock climber that's at the bottom of the rock right now looking up. You can't expect to just jump to the top. You got to move your way, climb your way back to the top. And you do that by focusing on the process, not the result. What do you need to do in order to execute a good shot? Focus on the breath. Focus on the steps that you need to take. So don't compare your current game to your best game. Start taking small action steps and climb back up that mountain. Go for it.
