We've all heard of comfort zones in relation to not wanting to practice, laziness, or being scared for change and growth. But a comfort zone can also show up in a sneaky way during your matches. It's a form of an expectation, and that's never a good thing. If you expect your match to go in a certain way because that's where you believe your levels at, then you're going to feel uncomfortable when all of a sudden you start playing better. You're not used to it, and you start to overthink. Suddenly you're focused on not making a mistake. You're losing focus because you're in the lead. You start making silly mistakes you weren't making before. Or you're losing confidence and start doubting yourself if you can finish this off. Being aware of this comfort zone is a huge first step. And step two is keep working the process. What do you need to do in order to make good shots? That's how you refocus your mind. Go for it. Good luck.
