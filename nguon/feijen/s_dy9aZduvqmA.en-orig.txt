Are you suffering from the payoff syndrome? It's when you make solid investments into your game like lessons and really hard practice and now you expect all this work to pay off in your next competitions. You see expectations are a big source of evil for our mental game because they create a lot of pressure. Expectations are the unwritten rules and standards and demands that a player puts on him or herself. I should win this match. I must reach the quarterfinals. I cannot fail and lose to this person. It's all fail or pass, good or bad, do or die. It doesn't work like that in competition. There are no guarantees. There's you giving yourself the best chance for success when you put in all the hard work. Enjoy the process and the progress and when it's time to compete, put the hammer down without any expectations. Go to work. Good luck.
