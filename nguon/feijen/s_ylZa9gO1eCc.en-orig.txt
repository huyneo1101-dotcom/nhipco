Are you suffering from the Fargo curse? Why even bother showing up at events anymore, right? Fargo has it all worked out for you anyways. More often than ever, I'm hearing these comments. He's a much higher Fargo. I have no chance. She's a much lower Fargo. I'm supposed to win easily. And now there's even score predictions of how matches should go between certain Fargo numbers. That's totally insane. It's like the future's already been written for us and players are buying into this. It's creating huge expectations and therefore pressure and a lot of frustration since when does a number behind somebody's name determine how a match is going to go. Look, it's not the best player that's going to win. It's the player that plays the best. You're playing the table and you're using your opponent as fuel to kick you into a higher gear. Break free from this chain and take responsibility for your own focus and the things that matter. Play the game and not the stats. Good luck.
