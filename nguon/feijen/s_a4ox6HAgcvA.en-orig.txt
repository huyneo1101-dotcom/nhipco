You can lose the match away from the pool table. What do I mean by that? Well, we all know these players that are shouting from the rooftops, I do everything for my sport. Well, are you really? How about your diet? Junk food or healthy food? Are you working out? Couch potato or healthy and fit? What are you watching? Dogs on skateboards or actually studying matches? How about your focus? Do you have a daily routine for training your mind like meditation or visualization or zero? What about your sleep? Are you reaching the recommended 7 to n hours a night or are you way under and really low on energy and screen time? Are you limiting yourself and having strong focus and presence or are you dumping all your mental energy and watching other people's lives? You see, you can practice all you want, but if this framework is lacking, you're going to have trouble reaching your full potential. So start making small changes in these departments and bring out the best of yourself
